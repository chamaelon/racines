Luttuɗum, dow duule ɗon woodi lesdi daga dow. Walaa kooɗume. O ɗon yi'a duniyaaru dow, o ƴiwoyi nder duule. O ɗon yi'a bee debbo maako duniyaaru ndu walaa goɗɗum, walaa daande, wala wolde. Ɗu'um waɗi kuuje ɗe cembiɗini dow leydi, nden ɓe puɗɗi wurtaago.

Ɗuum waɗi duuɓi ɗuuɗɗi, ɓe njotti hakkunde asama e duniyaaru. Yimɓe ɓe'e nder lesdi ladde, haa nokkuure nde Tibeel jooɗoto. Luttudu ɗon maaki. Walaa ko wuurtata to ndiyam walaa.

L'ancêtre : « Toowe daga lesdi dow, wari haa am! »

Sey duule ɗen udditi, duniyaaru boo nyaami. Sey ɓe nyiɓi nokkuure mawɗe, ɓe maɓɓiti gese. Kanjum woni gariiri arandeeji. Wakkati maajum, ɓikkoy maayi. Debbo'en ɓen hoo'i leydi e kaaƴe leɗɗe cukalel, ɓe ɓanngini mahol.

Kamɓe ɓe ngaɗi laabi daga dow ngam walaa mo wi'a to ɓe ƴiwoy.

Le vieux : « Baabiraawo meeɗen wari diga dow. »

Caggal ɗum, laamiiɗo Tibelel ɓadake.

L'enfant : « Non, moye waɗi walluuji ɗi'e? »

Hannde, rewɓe nder Tibeel ɗon ɓesda cuuɗi maɓɓe.
