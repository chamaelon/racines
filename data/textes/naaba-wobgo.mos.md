Yʋʋm kobs 19 wã saabẽ, a Ouagadougou ra yaa Mozi soolmã tẽn-kãsengã. A Buukary Koutou sẽn wa n yaa rĩmã, a pʋdame t'a Naaba Wobgo.

Yʋʋmd 1894 wã, a sigla taab ne Britãnsã tʋm-tʋmd a George Ekem Ferguson. A Frans nebã kɩɩsame, bala bãmb me rat n dɩka tẽn-kãnga.

Yʋʋmd 1896 wã, Paul Voulet ne a Julien Chanoine sull n kẽnga Mossi tẽngẽ. A Naaba Wobgo sẽn modgd n na n tõoge, b yõoga a tẽngã bugum. A Voulet rɩka Ouagadougou.

Yʋʋmd 1897 wã, a Voulet kɩtame t'a saam-biig a Sighiri lʋɩ a naamã. A Naaba Wobgo kosa Britani wã tɩ b sõng-a, n pa tõog ye. A zoe n kẽnga Gold Coast, sẽn yaa Ghana rũndã-rũndã wã.

Sẽn yɩɩd fãa, b pa sak n deeg bũmb nins fãa a sẽn yeelã ye. Yaa sɩd t'a goamã ket n yaa sɩda.
