Gãrango, sẽn yaa tẽn-kãseng a ye pʋgẽ wã, neb nins sẽn yaa kãsm dãmb segd n tũuda b noyã. A yaa kãsem soaba, la a pa zabd ne taab ye. Zu-soaba kiime, la tẽnga fãa yãbdame. La d zoe n get-a lame. Ned pa rat n gũ ye.

Le prétendant : « M pa rat ned ye. »

La a Zezi meng na n yeelg-a-la naam roogo. Kãsem-dãmbã wõgemda b ninã, n pa leok ye. Tãn-kãnga lʋɩɩsame n lʋɩ. A wʋka-a lame. A leb n lʋɩɩsame. Sa-kãseng yiime, la bõn-buudã lʋɩɩs pʋʋgẽ. Nebã zãaga b mens ne-a. B yetame tɩ sã n pa tar Lingãni rãmbã, b na n paama zu-soaba.

Rẽ poore, kãsem-dãmbã na n baoo taoor soab ning b sẽn yãkã. B tall-a lame n kẽng Tangare. Tangaré yaa Lingani nebã tẽn-tẽngã.

Le guide : « Ad-y ned ning sẽn watã. »

Sẽn yãk-a wã kẽnda zĩiga. Ned pa mi bũmb ning sẽn maand be ye. Sẽn paase, d pa na n togs nebã bũmb ning b sẽn maandã ye. Yaa wakat ning b sẽn na n dɩ naam wã. A Zezi naam roog n lʋɩɩ bãmb zugu, la b tõoga. La bilf-bilfu, saagã leb n waa tẽngã zugu.

Baa rũndã-rũndã, b yetame tɩ ned baa a yen ka rɩt naam Garango soolmẽ n yaool n kẽng Tangare.
