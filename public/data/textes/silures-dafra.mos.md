Kẽer sẽn be Houet kʋɩlgã pʋgẽ wã, koomã ra yaa wa ko-kãseng wakat fãa. Kẽer yaa zĩm-dãmb sẽn tar gãn-kãsemse. Daar a ye, b sẽn da wa n naan tẽngã, koglg ned sẽn da tar koomã yãee zĩ-kãng. A rɩka bõn-yũudr la a bas kũun n na n pʋʋs Wẽnnaam barka. Tẽnga sẽn be ko-kãsengã sɛɛgẽ wã yaa Sya, b sẽn da boond tɩ Bobo-Dioulasso.

Tẽngã neba ra wa n baooda koom Dafra, la b da pa yĩm ne zĩmã rɩɩb ye. A Awa, a Sya pagã ra pa tar bi-ribl ye. A sũur yɩɩ toog wʋsgo. Daar a ye koabgẽ, a kẽnga koglgã pʋga. A Dafra gũudã ra gũuda yẽnda be.

Awa : « Dafra Silures, m kot-y tɩ y kõ maam biiga. »

Mah : « Tẽeg-y y pʋlengã, la y gũ. »

Kẽer n yi koomã pʋgẽ. B wʋma pʋlemdã. La ka la bilfu, Awa rɩka a biigã. A leb n waame n na n pʋʋs a Zeova barka. La daar a ye, zĩm-biisi sẽn yit tẽn-zẽnga waame.

Mah : « Ka ned sẽn baood zĩm-gãong ye. Rɩt-kãens yaa bõn-sõamyã. »

A yãgsame. A lob-a lame n dɩ zĩm a ye. A kẽnga be, a sũur yɩɩ noog ne-a. A Zezi poore, saasẽ lebga lik. Rẽ n so tɩ koom-koglgã sa. A Sya pʋgẽ, b pa kõ-b bũmb ye. Neb nins sẽn da be tẽn-kãnga pʋgẽ wã yii koom koglgẽ n lebg n wa ne b nusã.

La zĩm-biisã sẽn da be zĩig sẽn zãre wã, bãaga yõk-b lame. A Zezi sẽn wa n zãad ne b nugã, a lebgame.

Le pêcheur : « Bas-y maam, gũudã. M da pa mi ye. »

A kɩtame tɩ zĩmã leb koomẽ. A Ma wãbda zĩm-dãmbã. La koomã lebg n waame. Tẽngã neb waame tao-tao n na n maan sũ-noogo. Rũndã-rũndã me, neb nins sẽn wa n be Dafra wã rɩtda rũmsã, la b kõt-b-la b pʋlemsã.
